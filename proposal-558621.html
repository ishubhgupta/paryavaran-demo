Our reply · 21/07/2026
The proposed 4-lane Southern Bypass to Jhansi, connecting NH-44 and NH-39, is proposed to accommodate increasing traffic and to improve connectivity for passenger and goods movement. The proposed greenfield road alignment bypasses Jhansi/Orchha towns and length of 15.512 km, including 11.494 Km in Niwari district (MP) and 4.018 Km in Jhansi district (UP) . The proposed road alignment has been finalized after thoroughly studied the entire area. The Orchha Wildlife Sanctuary and their Eco-Sensitive Zone has been avoided in the proposed alignment. However, the forest area cannot be completely avoided from the alignment, for the proposed alignment, 10.4240 ha of forest land is proposed for diversion. Total length of road in forest land is 1.737 Km. A total of 10.4240 ha of forest land is Reserve Forest is located at Km 12.961 to Km 14.698. From Design Km 0.000 to Km 2.332 (Bangay Khas to Majra Bangay) and from Design Km 6.350 to Km 15.512 (Gujjara Khurd to Pratap Pura), all within the Tikamgarh Forest Division. Between Design Km 2.332 to Km 6.350, the road passes through village Saiyar (Jhansi district), where no forest land is involved . The proposed road alignment passes through 12 villages in Orchha Tehsil (Niwari District) and One village in Jhansi Tehsil (Jhansi District). Total land required is 110.9387 ha, including 100.5147 ha non-forest and 10.4240 ha forest land . The proposed road alignment has been finalized is the best alignment and suits the existing topography and the barest minimum forest land diversion.

3
The project alignment involves 04 highly critical Extra High-Tension (EHT) line crossings, including 765 KV, 400 KV, 220 KV, and 132 KV lines between chainages 0+400 and 12+850. Additionally, the construction corridor intercepts 127 high-tension/low-tension electrical poles and 31 transformers. Clear commitment is required from the User Agency on whether the supplementary forest area required for tree felling or lopping along these utility clearance corridors has been subsumed within the present 10.24 ha diversion proposal, or if separate clearances will be sought.
Our reply
The proposed project alignment involves four (04) High Tension (HT) line crossings, comprising three (03) 132 kV transmission lines and one (01) 220 kV transmission line. These crossings required raising in height as per site condition. A total of eight (10) Low Tension (LT) power line crossings are involved along the proposed project alignment, comprising four (06) LT lines in Niwari District, Madhya Pradesh, and four (04) LT lines in Jhansi District, Uttar Pradesh. These LT lines are required to be shifted within the proposed utility corridor of proposed alignment. However, no supplementary forest land or tree felling is required for obtaining these utility clearances, as the utility corridor has already been accommodated within the proposed Right of Way (RoW). The No Objection Certificates (NOCs) issued by the Electrical Departments of Niwari District and Jhansi District are enclosed as Annexure-1 and Annexure-2 , respectively.
4
A review of the working plan 'Restoration' and 'Rehabilitation' circle schedules (specifically for the Bachhoda, Kashipura, Koti, and Lidhora treatment categories in the Orchha/Niwadi ranges) indicates that the target compartments, including RF-123 and RF-124, are strictly mapped under active state silvicultural operations. These compartments have designated, year-wise coupe management and regeneration interventions scheduled through the 2024–2025 to 2033–2034 working plan cycle. Because the permanent construction of a 4-lane national highway bypass will permanently fragment and subtract these treatment coupes from active forest production, a formal deviation assessment from the Working Plan officer regarding the long-term impact on regional canopy regeneration is necessary.
Our reply
With reference to the Working Plan of RF-123 and RF-124, we hereby undertake to comply with all the conditions that may be stipulated by the Working Plan Officer. In this regard, Undertaking is enclosed as Annexure-3 .

Our reply
The proposed alignment does not pass through the notified Eco-Sensitive Zone (ESZ) boundary of the Orchha Wildlife Sanctuary. However, the nearest point of the proposed alignment is located at a distance of 670 m from the notified ESZ boundary of the Orchha Wildlife Sanctuary. The KML file of the notified ESZ boundary is enclosed as Annexure-5 for reference. The proposed alignment traverses Reserved Forest (RF) Compartment Nos. RF-123 and RF-124 from Design Km 12+961 to 14+698, covering a total length of 1.737 km within forest land. To facilitate the movement of wildlife and minimize the project's ecological impact on the above said stretch of forest land, we have incorporated three (03) culverts at Design Km 13+100, 13+810 & 14+200, one (01) Rail Over Bridge (ROB) at Design Km 13+560, and one (01) major bridge at Design 14+675, which will serve as wildlife crossing structures and help maintain habitat connectivity along the project corridor. Wildlife Management Plan is enclosed as Annexure-6 .
